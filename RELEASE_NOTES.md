# What's New in this Release

- Separate Retail (Form 20/21) and Wholesale (Form 20B/21B) billing modes with distinct Drug License numbers
- Strictly prohibited loose medicine sales (khuchra bikri) in Wholesale Tax Invoices
- Full Balance Sheet & Ledger system tracking supplier dokan purchases, payments, and outstanding due balances
- Complete Customer medicine purchase history list and due balance settlement
- Expiry medicine returns module to return expired stock back to supplier with automatic debit note ledger adjustments
